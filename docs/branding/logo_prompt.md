# ECHAT — Logo / App Icon Prompt

## What the app is
ECHAT is a modern, real-time messaging app for Android and iOS (built with Flutter + Firebase).
People use it to chat one-on-one, share photos, videos, PDFs and documents, and make voice and
video calls. It shows WhatsApp-style ticks (sent, delivered, read), unread badges, online status and
"last seen", and keeps a history of calls inside each chat. It should feel fast, friendly, secure
and premium.

Brand colours:
- Primary violet gradient: #8E7CF8 → #4834D4 (dark mode: #7B6CF2 → #3B2DB8)
- Accent sky blue (read ticks): #7DD3FC
- Online green: #22C55E

## Prompt (copy into Midjourney / DALL·E / Ideogram / Firefly)
Design a premium mobile app icon for "ECHAT", a modern real-time chat and video-calling app.
A square app icon with rounded corners, filled with a smooth diagonal violet gradient from soft
lavender (#9D8CFF) in the top-left to deep indigo (#2E2296) in the bottom-right, with a gentle
light glow in the top-left corner. In the centre, a bold, clean white speech bubble with a small
tail at the bottom-left and a soft drop shadow. Inside the bubble, a thick, rounded, geometric
capital letter "E" in a violet gradient — its three arms look like lines of a text message.
In the top-right corner of the bubble, a small sky-blue (#7DD3FC) circular notification dot.
Flat vector style, minimal, bold shapes, crisp edges, no small details, no extra text, centred,
high contrast, readable at 48px, Apple / Google Play app-store quality, 1024×1024.

Negative prompt: realistic photo, 3D clutter, extra letters, words, watermark, thin lines,
busy background, multiple bubbles, drop-shadowed text, low resolution, blurry.

## Variations to try
- Wordmark: the icon on the left, "ECHAT" in a bold rounded geometric sans-serif (e.g. Poppins
  Bold / SF Pro Rounded) in deep indigo #2E2296 on the right, white background.
- Monochrome: white bubble + E on solid #4834D4, for notification icons and splash screens.
- Dark mode: same icon on a near-black (#0B0B12) tile with a violet glow behind the bubble.
